## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Information you give us
- **Account & profile:** full name, email address, phone number, gender
  (optional — including a "do not prefer" option), target exam group, and
  preferred language (English / Tamil / both).
- **Authentication:** a password (managed via our authentication provider,
  Supabase — we do not store it in plain text) or, if you use **Google
  Sign-In**, basic Google profile information (name, email and, if available,
  profile photo).
- **Payments:** when you buy Premium, we record the **order ID, payment ID,
  amount, currency, plan, status and any coupon used**. Your full card/UPI/bank
  details are collected and processed by **Razorpay**, not by us.
- **Communications you submit:** feedback and ratings, question
  error/correction reports, and support messages.

### 2.2 Information we generate from your use
- **Learning & performance data:** tests taken, questions attempted, answers
  selected, correct/incorrect results, scores, accuracy, time taken,
  per-subject/topic performance, bookmarks, spaced-revision items, daily
  activity, streaks, badges/levels (gamification), and test "violations"
  recorded during proctored tests (e.g. full-screen exits / tab switches).
- **Device & session data:** a generated **device identifier**, **user-agent
  (browser/device) string**, and active **device sessions** (to enforce the
  device limit and let you manage signed-in devices).
- **Push subscription data:** if you enable notifications, your browser's **Web
  Push subscription endpoint and keys**.

### 2.3 Technical & log data
Standard technical data such as IP address, request logs, timestamps and error
diagnostics, collected by us or our hosting providers to keep the Service
secure and reliable.

### 2.4 Local storage on your device
We store certain items locally in your browser, including **authentication
tokens, the device identifier, and your theme/language preference**, so the
Service works and remembers your settings. These are used for functionality and
security, not for third-party advertising.

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We use personal data to:

- track and display your progress, analytics, streaks and achievements;
- run proctored tests and maintain test integrity;
- process and verify payments and manage Premium entitlement (via Razorpay);
- send service, transaction, security and (with your consent) other
  notifications by email, in-app message and web push;
- provide support and respond to feedback and reports;
- improve the Service, including correcting reported questions and analysing
  aggregate usage;
- detect, prevent and address fraud, abuse, security incidents and violations
  of our Terms; and
- comply with legal obligations.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data,
including: passwords managed by our authentication provider (not stored in plain
text by us), payment credentials handled by the payment processor, server-side
verification of payments and answer keys (answer keys are not exposed to the
browser), access controls, and watermarking of downloadable PDFs to deter
unauthorised sharing. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure, and
we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a personal-data breach occurs, we will
notify the Data Protection Board and affected users as required by law.
